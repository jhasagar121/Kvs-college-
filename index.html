About This Portal

KVSC Study Materials is a student-run resource hub for Kalidas Vidyapati Science College, Uchhaith, Benipatti. It brings together assignments, notes, reference PDFs, previous year question papers and official notices in one simple, fast-loading page — built and maintained by Sagar for fellow students. No login, no app install, no cost. If a file you need isn't here yet, just send a WhatsApp message and it'll be added.

सवाल-जवाब

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this the official college website?

No — this is a student-maintained study materials portal for KVSC College students. For official college matters, contact the college office directly.

A PDF link isn't working. What do I do?

Tap the "Request on WhatsApp" button on that card, or use the floating WhatsApp button in the corner — mention the subject and semester and it'll be sent to you directly.

Can I request notes for a subject that isn't listed?

Yes. Message on WhatsApp or use the contact form below with the subject, semester and teacher's name if known.

Is my data saved anywhere?

No account or personal data is stored on a server. Dark mode preference is saved only in your own browser (localStorage).
